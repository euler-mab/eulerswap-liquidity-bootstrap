Swap depth — effective price & price impact vs trade size

Effective price is output-per-input normalized to the 1:1 peg. The curve stays flat near $1 (concentration ≈ 1) until the trade approaches the seeded inventory; this template is unleveraged, so the pool draws inventory down to the equilibrium reserves and does not borrow.
