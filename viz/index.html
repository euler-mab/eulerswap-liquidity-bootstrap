EulerSwap bootstrap market — visualizer

A read-only view of the ungoverned mini market and the EulerSwap pool that bootstraps it. The market structure renders offline; connect an RPC + the deployed pool for live reserves and the depth curve. Self-contained — no build, no server. See viz/README.md.

The market

Borrow / liquidation LTV for each collateral → borrowable pair (1e4 = 100%). The stable diagonal (e.g. USDC→USDC) is the leverage loop; WETH→WETH and BTC→BTC are correlated self-tiers at 0.90. Escrow and borrowable forms of an asset share a number — expanded to vault level that's the 63 pairs wired by _ltvs() in BootstrapMarketBase.sol.

Paste a mainnet RPC + the deployed pool address to load live reserves and depth.

Cumulative dollars tradable vs the marginal execution price, centred on the $1 peg — sells (green) push price below $1, buys (red) push it above; the gap at the centre is the swap-fee spread. Concentration ≈ 1 stacks almost all depth within a hair of $1, and the curve tops out at the seeded inventory because this template is unleveraged. Drag the sliders to feel how concentration and fee reshape it.
