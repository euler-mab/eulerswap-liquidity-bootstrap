Borrow / liquidation LTV for each collateral → borrowable pair (1e4 = 100%). The stable diagonal (e.g. USDC→USDC) is the leverage loop; off-diagonal stables are the cross. 31 pairs total — exactly the set wired by _ltvs() in BootstrapMarketBase.sol.

Liquidity depth — dollars available vs execution price

Cumulative dollars tradable as a function of the marginal execution price, centred on the $1 peg. The left arm sells the stable for USDC (price < 1); the right arm buys it (price > 1). Concentration ≈ 1 stacks almost all depth within a hair of $1; the curve tops out at the seeded inventory because this template is unleveraged.
