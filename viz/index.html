Liquidity depth — dollars available vs execution price

Cumulative dollars tradable as a function of the marginal execution price, centred on the $1 peg. The left arm sells the stable for USDC (price < 1); the right arm buys it (price > 1). Concentration ≈ 1 stacks almost all depth within a hair of $1; the curve tops out at the seeded inventory because this template is unleveraged (it draws inventory down to the equilibrium reserves and does not borrow).
